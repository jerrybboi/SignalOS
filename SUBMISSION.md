# SignalOS — Hackathon Submission Notes

## Track
Binance Agent OS Mini Hackathon — Track A

## One-line pitch
SignalOS is an AI market-decision agent that detects unusual Binance market conditions, investigates the evidence, builds testable theses, and checks contextual trade risk before the user acts.

## Golden demo path
1. Open Home and introduce the problem: traders have too much data and too little clarity.
2. Open Radar and show live ranked market conditions from Binance market data.
3. Investigate the highest-confidence signal with MoveLens.
4. Show the separation between observed data, derived metrics, and SignalOS interpretation.
5. Build/open the asset thesis and show VALID / WEAKENING / INVALID conditions.
6. Open Guard and evaluate a hypothetical 200 USDT trade.
7. End on the Guard explanation and the line: "SignalOS — From signal to decision."

## Suggested 60–90 second voiceover
"Crypto traders do not need another dashboard full of numbers. They need to know what deserves attention, why it matters, what would invalidate the idea, and whether acting now adds unnecessary risk. SignalOS turns live Binance market conditions into a four-stage decision workflow. Radar detects unusual activity. MoveLens investigates the evidence and surfaces both supporting and contradicting observations. Thesis turns that reasoning into explicit conditions that can be valid, weakening, or invalid. Guard then evaluates a hypothetical action against the current market state without executing a trade. SignalOS is decision support, not an autonomous trading bot. It separates observed Binance data from calculations and interpretation, and the hackathon V1 runs without paid APIs or trading capital. SignalOS: from signal to decision."
